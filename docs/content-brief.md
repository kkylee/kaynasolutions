## Voice

- Second person, plain English, professional and warm. Light Taglish only where it lands naturally; never as decoration.
- Outcome before technology. "Connect the tools you already use" — not "API orchestration".
- Every claim names a concrete artefact: an approval, a report, a synced record, a dashboard.
- No superlatives ("best", "cheapest", "world-class"), no guaranteed results, no invented history, team size, awards, certifications, clients, or years of experience.
- Filipino context appears as scenarios: payroll cutoffs, branch stock counts, approvals passing through chat, month-end reporting, records spread across spreadsheets and group chats.
- Sentence case for headings. No exclamation marks. No emoji in copy.

## Demonstration content

Projects and articles are sample content. Each project card carries a "Demonstration project" label; the Portfolio page carries a page-level note. The Insights page states the articles are sample content. Nothing may imply real client engagements.
