# Sol Digital brand assets

The mark is an original geometric symbol: two interlocking rounded links that form an S. It sits on a navy tile with a white link and a teal link. The lettering is set in Manrope: **Sol** at 600 and **Digital** at 400, with "Digital" at 70% opacity for hierarchy. The type is outlined, so the SVGs do not depend on an installed font.

## Typography

- **Manrope** (variable) — wordmark and display headings. The wordmark uses 600 for "Sol" and 400 for "Digital".
- **Inter** (variable) — body copy and interface text.

Both fonts are self-hosted by the website (Fontsource, latin subset).

## Clear space and minimum size

- Keep clear space of at least half the mark's height on every side. At the lockup's native 32 px height, that is 16 px.
- Minimum sizes: mark 24 px on screen or 8 mm in print; lockup 120 px wide on screen or 35 mm in print.
- Do not place the lockup smaller than the wordmark can be read.

## Usage

Do:

- Use the primary lockup on white or paper-coloured surfaces.
- Use the inverse lockup or the inverse mark on navy and other dark surfaces.
- Use the mark alone for favicons, app icons, and tight spaces.
- Keep the mark's proportions and internal spacing intact.

Do not:

- Stretch, rotate, skew, or outline the mark or wordmark.
- Recolour the logo or add gradients, shadows, glows, or strokes.
- Retype the wordmark in another font or respace the letters.
- Place the lockup over busy photography. Use the mark on a solid panel instead.
- Put the primary lockup on a dark background or the inverse lockup on a light one.

## Notes

- "Digital" is intentionally set at 70% opacity in both lockups, matching the site header. Keep the lockup on a flat surface; over photography, use the mark alone.
- `soldigital.com` in the site contact placeholders is a placeholder, not a confirmed domain.
- The site favicon (`public/favicon.svg`) reuses the mark's geometry.
